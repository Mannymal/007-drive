007 Drive privacy policy

007 Drive only accesses files in, or shared with, its own Google account.

File contents are processed on the owner's computer and sent to the AI model provider (Anthropic) when the assistant reads them. They may be kept in the assistant's local history and memory.

Data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Only the owner uses this app, and they can remove access at any time by unsharing files or revoking the app in their Google account.
